# Daylily Catalog Goals

## Overarching goals

- Make Daylily Catalog useful and reliable for daylily growers and members.
- Increase paid membership by helping growers create useful catalogs and keep using them.
- Keep the project simple and safe for agents to improve.

## Current priorities (2026-09-27)

1. **Agent quality:** Use consistent patterns, clear boundaries, and reliable verification. Reduce rework and regressions.
2. **Paid-member growth:** Help qualified growers reach a useful catalog, make a paid membership decision, and keep using the product.

Judge growth by paid conversion, activation, and retention. Traffic, SEO
impressions, imports, and captures are leading indicators. Replace completed
or superseded current priorities when the user changes direction.
